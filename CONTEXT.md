# Personal Momentum OS (Master Chef Context)

A self-directed momentum and trajectory tracking system modeled on a high-output master kitchen, measuring effort applied to life stations and the velocity of finished milestones.

## Language

### Hierarchy

**Life Direction**:
The overarching culinary menu sections of the user's life (e.g., Craft & Engineering, Physical Vitality, Capital & Wealth).
_Avoid_: Area, Category, Domain, Pillar

**Priority**:
A persistent kitchen station or burner dedicated to a long-term discipline.
_Avoid_: Project, Moon, Goal, Category

**Milestone**:
A finite, sequential order ticket or dish currently cooking at a station with a mandatory quantitative target and unit.
_Avoid_: Goal, Task, Todo, Milestone without target

---

### Station Heat (Priority States)

**High Heat**:
The front burner receiving active, high-intensity focus and cognitive priority.
_Avoid_: Cook, Active, In Progress

**Simmer**:
The back burner receiving steady, low-maintenance attention to hold temperature and prevent skill decay.
_Avoid_: Consolidate, Passive, Maintenance

**Prep**:
A station undergoing recipe research, mise en place, ingredient sourcing, or experimentation before committing to high flame.
_Avoid_: Explore, Planning, Scoping

**Pantry**:
An off-duty station stored away in cold storage; preserved recipes with no current active heat.
_Avoid_: Archive, Inactive, Dormant

---

### Heat Vectors (Effort Actions)

**Mise en Place**:
A friction-reducing setup touchpoint, quick reading, environment prep, or tool sharpening (<15 min).
_Avoid_: Spark, Micro-task, Prep work

**Sear**:
A focused, high-intensity burst of execution or sprints (30–60 min).
_Avoid_: Fire, Sprint, Quick session

**Deep Simmer**:
A prolonged, uninterrupted deep work session tackling complex architecture or rigorous practice (90 min+).
_Avoid_: Cook Session, Deep work, Long session

**Tasting Note**:
An extracted conceptual insight, architectural synthesis, mental model breakthrough, or retrospective observation.
_Avoid_: Synthesis, Reflection, Journal entry

**Plate**:
A tangible intermediate artifact, draft, or public deliverable placed on the pass for the world to see (e.g. blog post, PR, spec).
_Avoid_: Serve (ambiguous with dish completion), Ship, Output

**Order Fulfilled**:
The terminal event marking the successful completion of an active quantitative milestone dish.
_Avoid_: Goal Achieved, Completed, Closed

**Scrapped Dish**:
An abandoned or missed milestone explicitly dumped into the Waste Ledger with a mandatory post-mortem note rather than silently modifying targets.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, Deleted, Edited milestone
